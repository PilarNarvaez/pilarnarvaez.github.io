Fire Walk With Me

I recreated Twin Peaks' iconic opening as a tribute to David Lynch's work. Some scenes are flooded with light, an intentional reference to Fire Walk With Me, using Video Copilot's Saber plug-in to achieve a luminous glow. The goal was to create an abstract, experimental feel, inspired by László Moholy-Nagy's Light Space Modulator. The music is a cover by Xiu Xiu.

Godard: Poster tributes to Histoire(s) du cinéma

I watched the Jean-Luc Godard documentaries Histoire(s) du cinéma and I loved them. Inspired by them I created these collages as a tribute to his great work using stills captured from the videos. There is one collage for each episode and the last collage is done with stills from the JLG and Anne-Marie Miéville documentary Deux fois cinquante ans de cinéma français. About

I'm a video editor and motion graphics artist with experience in localizing advertising, TV, and film campaigns, creating social media content, and producing brand campaigns and television promos.
I work primarily with Adobe Creative Suite (Premiere, After Effects) and Avid Media Composer. As a bilingual (Spanish/English) professional, I can work with multilingual content, including French.
Known for being a fast learner, hard worker, and detail-oriented, I'm dedicated to delivering high-quality results and thriving in creative, fast-paced environments.
